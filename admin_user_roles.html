How It Works

  • Primary Role: Users automatically get their primary role from their email when they log in.
  • Additional Roles: You can assign extra roles here to give users access to more features.
  • Combined Access: Users will have access to all routes allowed by any of their roles.
  • Example: A teacher can be given an Admin role to access admin features without changing their staff code.
